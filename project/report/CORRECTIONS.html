What caused them

  1. Cell lattice did not tessellate. Nothing crashed, the CSV looked fine, the render still read as a honeycomb. Found by eye in the gallery.
  2. A CSV grew a column mid-sweep. 22-field rows under a 21-field header; every later read shifted silently.
  3. A geometry parameter was never recorded. Left at its default, absent from params_json, indistinguishable from being set.
  4. Mean where the worst case was meant.
  5. Measurements typed into report templates. Third occurrence.
  6. Extruded families had no margin along the extrusion axis — they were measuring their own edge. Affects phase 1 only; no 3-D family uses that path.

What now blocks a repeat

  1. Gate check 7 — a template may not contain a literal that looks like a measurement. It found three more the moment it was written.
  2. Gate check 8 — every new sweep must re-measure a design an older sweep already holds, and agree per seed. It first reported "all agreeing" while skipping the one pair that disagreed, because seed was inside the design fingerprint. Fixed: 10 → 33 compared pairs.
  3. Tessellation assert — a cell array that does not tile is now a build error.
  4. Header guard — appending to a CSV whose header differs from the writer's fields refuses to run.
Unchanged by any of this: the phase 2/3/4 rankings of the 3-D families, which never used the extruded path — the cone control re-measured to ±0.0 % and the other 3-D families to ~1e-7 relative, the last bit of a float32 buffer.
Still true of every number in all three reports: nothing has been built or measured physically, and two chosen-not-measured coating properties set every absolute level. Ratios between designs are robust; percentages are not.
